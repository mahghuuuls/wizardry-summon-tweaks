# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

## [1.0.0]

### Added

- Suppress affected summons' own item and XP death drops, including supplied equipment. Enemies they defeat keep their normal rewards.
- Prevent affected summons from targeting players and block supported summon-versus-player attacks in both directions. Deliberate sacrifice, dismissal and natural expiry remain available.
- Toggle loot suppression and each direction of combat protection independently.
- Inspect a creature's classification, owner and active rules with `/wizardrysummontweaks inspect`.

### Compatibility

- Recognize supported player-owned spell and magical-staff summons from multiple mods.
- Preserve threat participation for allowed attacks in active [Threatworks](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/threatworks) 1.1.0 encounters.
